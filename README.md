# Pet Partner

## **Abstract**
Pet Partner is a user-friendly platform that helps individuals find the perfect pet that matches their personality, lifestyle, and preferences. It consists of four key sections:

- **Choose Your Best Buddy**: A questionnaire-based feature that suggests pets best suited to the user's lifestyle.
- **Track Pet Health**: Allows pet owners to log health data for routine care.
- **Safety Measures for Animals**: Educates users on essential pet care topics.
- **Available Pets for You**: Helps users locate pets for adoption in their area.

The platform fosters responsible pet ownership by providing resources and tools for both new and experienced pet owners.

## **1. Introduction**
Pet Partner connects people with pets suited to their lifestyle, simplifying adoption through personalized suggestions. The four sections provide tools for finding pets, monitoring health, learning responsible care, and discovering local adoption options.

## **2. Choose Your Best Buddy**
Users answer a questionnaire to receive pet recommendations based on their living conditions, activity levels, and preferences. Key features include:

- A lifestyle-based quiz
- Consideration of pet care time, personality, and space
- Matching users with suitable pets

## **3. Track Pet Health**
This feature enables pet owners to monitor their pet’s health by tracking diet, hydration, and activity levels. It also offers health tips and reminders for vet check-ups.

## **4. Safety Measures for Animals**
This section educates users on responsible pet handling, covering topics like tail docking, ear cropping, and dehorning. It promotes ethical practices and informed decisions.

## **5. Available Pets for You**
Users can search for adoptable pets by location, type, and breed. It includes:

- A map for locating adoption centers
- Detailed pet profiles
- A shopping section for pet products

## **Use Cases**
Pet Partner serves first-time owners, busy professionals, families, allergy sufferers, and adoption seekers. It also supports pet shelters and rescue organizations.

## **6. Future Steps**
Plans include AI-driven personalization, a mobile app, real-time pet health tracking, expanded adoption networks, and community features.

## **7. Technical Specifications**
The platform uses **HTML**, **CSS** for front-end design, and **JavaScript** for backend processing.

## **8. Features & Functionalities**
Pet Partner offers:

- A personality quiz for pet matching
- Health tracking and reminders
- Adoption search and pet profiles
- Educational resources on pet care
- A shopping feature for pet accessories
